Homes Protector ("we", "us", "our") helps homeowners request estimates for home improvement and remodeling projects. This policy explains what personal information we collect on this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website or submitting the estimate form, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you submit the "Get a Free Estimate" form:

  • Full name, phone number and email address
  • The home service you are interested in
  • Your US ZIP code and preferred project timeline
  • Any project details you choose to write
  • Your agreement to be contacted about your request

Please do not include sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank or card details, or passwords in the form.

Our hosting provider and the third-party services listed below may also receive basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit when your browser loads this site.

2. How we use your information

  • To review your estimate request and contact you about your project
  • To confirm whether service is available in your ZIP code
  • To match your request with the right home improvement professionals
  • To answer your questions and improve our website and services
  • To keep records and meet legal obligations

3. Who we share it with

We share your information only as needed to handle your request:

  • Service providers that help us run the website and receive form submissions, such as our form-processing and email providers.
  • Home improvement professionals and partners who may contact you to discuss or quote your project.
  • Legal and safety requests when disclosure is required by law, or needed to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • Business transfers if we are involved in a merger, sale or reorganization.

We do not publish your personal information on this website.

4. Calls, texts and email

When you check the consent box on the form, you agree that we, and professionals working on your request, may contact you by phone, text message or email about your estimate, including with automated technology, at the number and email address you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply to texts.

You can opt out at any time by telling the person who contacts you, replying STOP to a text message, or using the unsubscribe link in an email.

5. Third-party services on this site

  • Formspree processes the estimate form and forwards submissions to us.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts used on this site, which means your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google.
  • Unsplash hosts some of the photos shown on this site.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visits as described in its own privacy statement.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control how they handle data.

6. Retention and security

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your request, keep business records and meet legal requirements, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may also have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know, delete, correct, or opt out of certain sharing of your information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

You can also stop calls, texts and emails at any time, as described in section 4.

8. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, send us a message through the estimate form on our home page and mention "Privacy request" in the project details.
